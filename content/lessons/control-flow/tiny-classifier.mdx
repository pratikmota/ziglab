The starter already counts `n` from 1 through 4 with `while`. Your job is the **classifier**.

For each `n`, print `o` if it is odd and `e` if it is even. No spaces between letters. After the loop, the starter prints a newline.

You need `if` inside `while`. You do not need `for` or an array.
